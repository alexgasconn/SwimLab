These are locally calculated efforts, not Garmin's official personal records. Fastest session requires at least 100 m.

Click a column heading to sort. SPM = strokes / moving minutes. SPL = strokes / active lengths. DPS = metres / stroke. TPL = moving seconds / length. LPM = active lengths / moving minutes. Stroke percentages use all recorded active-length distance, including unclassified lengths. Missing metrics are shown as --, never zero-filled.

These filters affect the logbook and CSV export, not the dashboard charts.

Data quality & exclusions

Conservative physical limits and robust pace deviations flag sessions, not delete them. Unusual does not mean wrong: technique drills and long rests may be legitimate. Include / exclude decisions are saved locally. Invalid FIT lengths never qualify for records.

Import coverage & warnings
SWIMLAB / Independent personal dashboard, not affiliated with Garmin. Local, unencrypted health and optional GPS route data: protect this computer and its backups. Never expose this server to the Internet.
