04 / SWIM LOGBOOK

Every metric. One place.

0 SESSIONS

Click a column heading to sort. SPM = strokes / moving minutes; tempo = 60 / SPM in seconds per recorded stroke. Garmin's stroke-count convention is retained, with no assumed doubling; tempo is a same-arm cycle only if the device counts it that way. SPL = strokes / active lengths. DPS = metres / stroke. TPL = moving seconds / length. LPM = active lengths / moving minutes. Stroke percentages use all recorded active-length distance, including unclassified lengths. Missing metrics are shown as --, never zero-filled.

These filters affect the logbook and CSV export, not the dashboard charts.

Column groups

Date stays visible: click it to open the activity. Display options do not change CSV or AI exports. Options remain active until you reload this page.

Green = favorable, yellow = middle, red = unfavorable for the selected target, relative to each column's filtered minimum and maximum (three equal ranges). This is not an absolute performance or health grade. Compare similar styles, pool lengths and workouts. Missing or identical values stay uncolored.

Color targets by metric

Defaults favor more distance, moving ratio, DPS, LPM and SPM, and less pace time, SPL, TPL, tempo and SWOLF. Average temperature favors lower values, as selected; stroke percentages favor more presence, not better technique. Counts and heart rate have no default target. SPM, tempo and temperature have no universal ideal: adjust these targets to your workout. These are configurable comparisons, not training or health prescriptions.

Data quality & exclusions

Conservative physical limits and robust pace deviations flag sessions, not delete them. Unusual does not mean wrong: technique drills and long rests may be legitimate. Include / exclude decisions are saved locally. Invalid FIT lengths never qualify for records.
